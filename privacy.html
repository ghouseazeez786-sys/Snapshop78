Privacy Policy for SnapShop AI

Last updated: August 10, 2026

SnapShop AI ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is an instant AR shopping application that lets you snap a photo, preview products, compare prices, and access services such as hospital search, job listings, and government scheme information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By using SnapShop AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and phone number, if you register or sign in using Email/Password, Google, Facebook, Apple, or phone number (OTP) sign-in.
  • Profile and usage data — your selected plan (Free/Premium), app activity such as pages visited, features used, and preferences you set within the App.
  • Photos and camera data — images captured through the AI Camera or AR Try-On features, used only to generate previews and comparisons within the App on your device or session.
  • Location data (if permission is granted) — used to show nearby hospitals, price comparisons, or relevant local results.
  • Device and log information — general technical information such as browser/app version and crash logs, used to maintain and improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To provide core features: AR try-on previews, price comparisons, hospital search, job listings, government scheme information, and AI chat assistance.
  • To process Premium plan subscriptions and purchases, where applicable.
  • To improve, secure, and troubleshoot the App.
  • To communicate with you about your account or updates to the App, where necessary.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

SnapShop AI uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore (Google) — used to securely manage sign-in and store account/profile data. See Firebase's privacy practices.
  • Google Play Billing — used to process in-app purchases and subscriptions, where applicable.

These providers may collect and process data under their own privacy policies, in addition to this one.

4. Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored securely using Firebase's cloud infrastructure. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Health, Job, and Government Data

Certain features of the App display information about hospitals, job listings, and government schemes. This information is provided for general reference purposes only. SnapShop AI does not provide medical advice, guarantee employment outcomes, or act on your behalf with any government agency.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may review and update your account information within the App.
  • You may log out or delete your account at any time; contact us using the details below to request deletion of your account data.
  • You may disable camera or location permissions at any time through your device settings, though this may limit certain features.

7. Children's Privacy

SnapShop AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
